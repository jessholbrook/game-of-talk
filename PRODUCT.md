# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Speakers running 20 to 30 minute talks from a laptop in rooms with unpredictable lighting, projectors, and screen arrangements. Their primary job is to start a reliable ambient visualization before speaking, keep it visible beside slides or full-screen, and finish with a visual artifact shaped by the talk.

## Product Purpose

Game of Talk turns local microphone energy into an abstract, evolving cellular world. It never transcribes, records, uploads, or illustrates the speaker's words. Voice qualities act as environmental forces on a Life-inspired field. Every minute leaves behind a fossil block, and the completed talk becomes a mosaic of those moments.

## Brand Personality

Alive, enigmatic, composed. The work should feel like an instrument rather than presentation software: quiet before speech, responsive without being jumpy, and distinctive enough to hold attention without competing with the speaker.

## Anti-references

- Literal speech visualization, captions, waveforms, spectrograms, or word clouds
- Generic analytics dashboards and decorative control panels
- Retro terminal parody, hacker clichés, and gratuitous neon
- Motion that reacts to every syllable or becomes exhausting over a long talk
- Effects that require a network connection or reveal the content of the talk

## Design Principles

1. Voice becomes climate, not content.
2. The field should reward sustained attention and remain beautiful in silence.
3. Talk-day operation must be calm, legible, and reversible.
4. Every minute should leave evidence without interrupting the live field.
5. Companion mode and full-screen mode are equal presentations of the same world.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA for controls and text. Never rely on color alone for state. Respect reduced-motion preferences by limiting nonessential interface transitions while keeping the meaningful cellular simulation available. Preserve strong contrast for inconsistent projectors and provide a non-microphone demo mode.
